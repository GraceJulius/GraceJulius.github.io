01 — About

The practitioner-researcher.

I'm a senior Computer Science major (cybersecurity) at Lincoln University of Pennsylvania — the nation's first degree-granting HBCU — and a first-generation graduate-school applicant carrying a 3.98 GPA.

My work lives at the intersection of human-centered AI and cybersecurity: how people perceive, trust, and reason with intelligent systems — and how to make those interactions fair, transparent, and secure. I've built a multimodal reasoning benchmark to probe where vision-language models break down, designed an Ethical AI Dashboard analyzing how students perceive AI, and studied the human attack surface that social engineering exploits.

I move between the red-team curiosity of breaking things — responsibly disclosing an exposed API key, placing 4th of 50 in an international CTF — and the governance mindset of making systems safe at scale through threat modeling and security awareness programs that actually changed behavior on my campus.

The long game: a PhD in cybersecurity, where human-centered AI and security meet. This summer I join MIT's Multisensory Intelligence group as an MSRP research scholar. Off the clock — a camera or a tennis court.
